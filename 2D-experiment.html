What are we testing?

Whether a person's crisis decisions differ with a planning assistant available. Each paired session contains two human-operated rounds. Controls, budget, initial state, arrival noise and sensor-failure schedule are matched; responses change the subsequent state.

What the assistant is

A simulation-based planning agent, not a trained model or LLM. It evaluates legal actions over 36 simulated seconds using three hypotheses about water levels and rainfall. It receives reported sensor readings and history, station status, queues and resources—not true water depth, future noise or the hidden sensor-fault flag. Its transparent scoring balances modeled exposure, service, spending and information gained by inspecting conflicting readings. It can be wrong.

How outcomes are defined

Hazard exposure: queued riders × hazard intensity × simulated time, summed across stations. Hazard intensity uses flood depth and overcrowding, with response-team mitigation. It is a model exposure measure, not injuries or unique people harmed. Service share: transported riders / all generated riders, including starting queues. Evacuated and diverted riders are tracked separately. Response time: active seconds until the first successful action.

Simulation assumptions

An eight-stop teaching network inspired by NYC, with intermediate stops omitted. Water spreads along simplified connections. Rainfall, elevations, drainage, capacities, budgets, costs, thresholds and response durations are gameplay/model assumptions, not validated MTA engineering data. Throughput is modeled at station level; individual trains are not simulated. The sensor failure is the same in both replays.

Limits of the comparison

A repeated scenario introduces practice effects. Randomizing order across people helps counterbalance order, but one pair cannot establish a causal effect. Pauses and resumed sessions are recorded. Collect independent participants, balance order, predefine outcomes and test multiple cases before claiming a general benefit. No result is preselected or guaranteed.

Local data

Sessions and decisions stay in this browser's local storage. JSON export includes actions, outcomes, samples, order, pauses and the planner's score definition. No account, external service or API key is used.
